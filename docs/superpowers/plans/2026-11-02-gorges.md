# Gorges and hillslopes (v1.2) Implementation Plan

> Executed inline (superpowers:executing-plans). This file is also the ledger: ticks and `Ruling:` lines are appended as work lands.

**Goal:** Make incising rivers and the gorges they cut behave and look natural: loops keep growing and cutting off (slowly) in a gorge, loops cut off while incising are left hanging, valley walls slump, creep, fail in landslides, grow gullies and fans, collect scree, and show stepped rock layers.

**Request (Steven, 2026-11-02):** "oxbow lakes seem to stop or slow massively as vertical erosion takes hold ... can we factor in any other naturally occurring behaviours ... landslides? some of these gorges look unnatural" — then "skip the questions, just do it all".

**Architecture:** All new physics lives in `src/engine/hillslope.js` (creep, strata, landslides, gullies, dams' pond fill) called from `Engine.tick()` on land-update ticks, and only while the river is cutting (`incision > 0 || knickpoints.length`), so default rivers and the golden test are untouched. Renderer gains strata/scree colouring in `terrain.js` and a grid-based pond mesh in `src/render/pond.js`.

**Tech stack:** existing (vanilla JS, three.js, vitest, Playwright scripts).

## Global constraints
- Engine determinism: only `dmath.js` maths and the engine rng; the determinism guard test must stay green.
- Nothing new runs unless the river is cutting; golden pins for seeds 1–3 must not change.
- Saves from v1.1 (SAVE_VERSION 1) must still open; new state fields are optional with defaults and validated against hostile input.
- Medium-quality tick cost must not grow by more than ~1 ms on average while cutting.
- Plain-language UI copy, British spelling, in the style of the existing EXPLANATIONS/HELP.

## Tasks
- [ ] 1. Gorge migration: tall/rock banks slow migration less (floor on the combined factor), so a river incising at 4 m/kyr keeps cutting off loops and one at 15 m/kyr still winds. Under the river the bedrock is planed to the bed (strath), so the gorge floor reads as alluvium, not rock streaks. Test: `test/gorge.test.js` cutoffs continue between years 3000–7000 at 4 m/kyr; sinuosity > 1.3 at 15 m/kyr by year 6000; bedrock under channel cells ≤ bed.
- [ ] 2. Hillslopes: soil creep rounds rims (nonlinear, only on slopes steeper than scroll-bar relief); bedrock in alternating hard/soft layers (hard stand steeper) giving ledges; material shed from rock faces lands as scree (SURFACE.SCREE). Test: synthetic-grid tests in `test/hillslope.test.js`.
- [ ] 3. Landslides: random failures of tall walls (chance grows with wall height), scar cut back into the rim, debris lobe on the floor (volume conserved within 15%), scree surface, event + toast; debris reaching the river pushes the channel away for a few decades. "Landslide" button triggers one on the tallest wall. Tests in `test/hillslope.test.js`.
- [ ] 4. Landslide dams: a lobe that spans a narrow gorge floor dams the river; water upstream rises to the crest (flat pool, no migration in it), a lake fills the gorge floor (pond mask by flood fill), the dam erodes and sometimes fails suddenly with an outburst flood. Tests: level raised upstream, pond cells, breach over time, outburst event.
- [ ] 5. Gullies and fans: side gullies start at tall walls and cut headward into the plateau (V-shaped, concave profile), building a fan at the mouth. Tests: gully lengthens, lowers cells along it, fan raises cells at mouth.
- [ ] 6. Hanging loops: an oxbow cut off while the river is incising drains once the river has cut more than ~1.5 m below it, leaving a dry abandoned loop on the terrace ("abandoned meander" label). Test in `test/gorge.test.js`.
- [ ] 7. Saves: serialize/restore landslide pushes, dams, gullies, hanging loops; validateState checks them; v1.1 saves still open. Tests in `test/save.test.js`, `test/save-hostile.test.js`.
- [ ] 8. Renderer and UI: strata bands (buff hard cliffs, red-brown soft slopes), scree, fresh scar colour, pond mesh, explain labels (landslide, scree, gully, fan, landslide lake, abandoned meander, rock ledges), help text, Landslide button + touch, toasts.
- [ ] 9. Verify: full test suite, determinism script (Chromium/Firefox/WebKit vs Node), tick-cost probe, screenshots of a gorge before/after into docs/looks, README + docs/looks/README notes.
- [ ] 10. Fresh whole-branch review (opus), fix pass, commit, push, deploy, live smoke test.
